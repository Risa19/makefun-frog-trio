Pondlight

Recorded live at the Lily Pad, where the trio holds the Tuesday room. Marsh leans into the melody on tenor like he's testing the water; Reed walks the bass line low and slow; Tad keeps the brushes so soft you can hear the room breathe. They close every night with the same ballad, slower each time, until the whole room is holding its breath. Three sets, one pond, no overdubs.
